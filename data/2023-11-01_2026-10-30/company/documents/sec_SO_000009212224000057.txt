Item 8.01.
Other Events  .
On May 6, 2024, The Southern Company (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the upsize and pricing of an offering of $1.3 billion aggregate principal amount of its Series 2024A 4.50% Convertible Senior Notes due June 15, 2027 (the “Convertible Notes”) in a private offering to persons reasonably believed to be qualified institutional buyers pursuant to Rule 144A under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, reflecting an upsize of $200 million over the previously announced offering size.  In addition, the Company granted the initial purchasers an option to purchase, for settlement within a period of 13 days from, and including, the date the Convertible Notes are first issued, up to an additional $200 million aggregate principal amount of Convertible Notes.
A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated by reference herein.
