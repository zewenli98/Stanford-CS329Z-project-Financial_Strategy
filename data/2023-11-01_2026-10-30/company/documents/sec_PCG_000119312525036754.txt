Item 8.01. Other Events
On February 24, 2025, Pacific Gas and Electric Company (the “Utility”) entered into an Underwriting Agreement for the issue and sale of (i) $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.700% First Mortgage Bonds due 2035 (the “2035 Bonds”) and (ii) $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 6.150% First Mortgage Bonds due 2055 (the “2055 Bonds”, together with the 2035 Bonds, the “Mortgage Bonds”).
