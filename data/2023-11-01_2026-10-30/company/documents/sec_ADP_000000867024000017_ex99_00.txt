ADP Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2024 Results; Provides Fiscal 2025 Outlook
•  Revenues increased 7% to $19.2 billion for the year; 6% organic constant currency
•  Employer Services new business bookings increased 7% for the year to $2.0 billion
•  Net earnings increased 10% to $3.8 billion for the year, and adjusted net earnings increased 11% to $3.8 billion
•  Adjusted EBIT increased 9% to $4.9 billion for the year, and adjusted EBIT margin increased 70 basis points to 25.5%
•  Diluted earnings per share ("EPS") increased 11% to $9.10 for the year; adjusted diluted EPS increased 12% to $9.18
•  Fiscal 2025 consolidated outlook includes revenue growth of 5% to 6%, adjusted EBIT margin expansion of 60 to 80 basis points, and adjusted diluted EPS growth of 8% to 10%
ROSELAND, N.J. – July 31, 2024   – ADP (Nasdaq: ADP), a leading global technology company providing human capital management (HCM) solutions, today announced its fourth quarter and fiscal 2024 financial results and provided its fiscal 2025 outlook.
Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2024 Consolidated Results
Compared to last year’s fourth quarter, revenues increased 6% to $4.8 billion and 7% on an organic constant currency basis. Net earnings increased 7% to $829 million, and adjusted net earnings increased 10% to $858 million. Adjusted EBIT increased 10% to $1.1 billion, representing an adjusted EBIT margin increase of 80 basis points in the quarter to 23.3%. ADP’s effective tax rate for the quarter was 23.9% on both a reported basis and an adjusted basis. Diluted EPS increased 8% to $2.02, and adjusted diluted EPS increased 11% to $2.09.
For the full year, revenues increased 7% to $19.2 billion, 6% organic constant currency. Net earnings increased 10% to $3.8 billion, and adjusted net earnings increased 11% to $3.8 billion. Adjusted EBIT increased 9% to $4.9 billion, resulting in adjusted EBIT margin expansion of 70 basis points to 25.5%. ADP's full year tax rate was 23.0% on both an effective basis and an adjusted basis. Diluted EPS increased 11% to $9.10, and adjusted diluted EPS increased 12% to $9.18, including a net share count reduction.
“Our strong fourth quarter results concluded another very successful year for ADP," said Maria Black, President and Chief Executive Officer, ADP. “Employer Services new business bookings growth came in at the high-end of our expectations, and client satisfaction reached a new all-time high for the year. These results reflect the power of our innovative HCM solutions and the collective efforts of our talented associates who deliver unmatched service for our clients each day. As we look ahead, we remain focused on executing against our strategic priorities and continuing to lead the way in workforce innovation."
"Consistent execution on our proven business model produced strong revenue and earnings growth in fiscal 2024," said Don McGuire, Chief Financial Officer, ADP. "Our fiscal 2025 guidance anticipates ongoing solid revenue growth, Employer Services new business bookings growth, and margin expansion as we continue to reinvest in our business to drive long-term profitable growth for our shareholders."

Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2024 Segment Results
Employer Services   – Employer Services offers a comprehensive range of global HCM and Human Resources Outsourcing solutions. Compared to last year:
•  Employer Services revenues increased 7% on a reported basis and 7% on an organic constant currency basis for the fourth quarter, and increased 8% on a reported basis and 7% on an organic constant currency basis for the fiscal year
•  Employer Services new business bookings increased 7% to $2.0 billion for the fiscal year
•  Employer Services client revenue retention decreased to 92.0% for the fiscal year, from 92.2%
•  U.S. pays per control increased 2% for the fourth quarter and for the fiscal year
•  Employer Services segment margin increased 220 basis points for the fourth quarter and increased 210 basis points for the fiscal year
PEO Services   – PEO Services provides comprehensive employment administration outsourcing solutions. Compared to last year:
•  PEO Services revenues increased 6% for the fourth quarter and increased 4% for the fiscal year
•  PEO Services revenues excluding zero-margin benefits pass-throughs increased 7% for the fourth quarter and increased 3% for the fiscal year
•  Average worksite employees paid by PEO Services increased 3% to about 742,000 for the fourth quarter and increased 2% to about 729,000 for the fiscal year
•  PEO Services segment margin decreased 240 basis points for the fourth quarter and decreased 150 basis points for the fiscal year
Included within the results of our segments above:
Interest on Funds Held for Clients   – The safety, liquidity, and diversification of ADP clients’ funds are the foremost objectives of the Company’s investment strategy. Client funds are invested in accordance with ADP’s prudent and conservative investment guidelines, and most of the investment portfolio is rated AAA/AA. Compared to last year:
•  Interest on funds held for clients increased 17% to $277 million for the fourth quarter and increased 26% to $1.0 billion for the fiscal year
•  Average client funds balances increased 4% to $36.1 billion for the fourth quarter and increased 4% to $35.4 billion for the fiscal year
•  The average interest yield on client funds increased 30 basis points to 3.1% for the fourth quarter and increased 50 basis points to 2.9% for the fiscal year
