Section 5 - Corporate Governance and Management
Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
Carrier Global Corporation (the “Company”) held its 2024 Annual Meeting of Shareowners on April 18, 2024. As of February 27, 2024, the record date for the meeting, 900,102,917 shares of the Company’s common stock were issued and outstanding. A quorum of 828,944,881 shares of common stock was present or represented at the meeting.
