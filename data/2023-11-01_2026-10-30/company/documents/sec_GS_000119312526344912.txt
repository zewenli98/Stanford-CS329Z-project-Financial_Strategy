Item 5.03
Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.
On August 11, 2026, the Company filed a Certificate of Elimination to its Restated Certificate of Incorporation with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware eliminating from the Restated Certificate of Incorporation all matters set forth in the Certificate of Designations with respect to its 3.65% Fixed-Rate Reset  Non-Cumulative  Preferred Stock, Series U (the “Series U Preferred Stock”).
