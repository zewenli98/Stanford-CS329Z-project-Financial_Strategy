Item 5.02    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On December 5, 2023, Kenvue Inc. (the "Company") announced that Kathleen Widmer, Group President, North America and Latin America, will transition out of Kenvue effective December 31, 2023.
This is an involuntary termination without "cause" within the meaning of the Executive Severance Pay Plan of Kenvue Inc. and U.S. Affiliated Companies (the "Severance Plan") and Ms. Widmer's long-term incentive and other awards. Accordingly, subject to her execution of a release of claims in favor of the Company and its affiliates and her continuing compliance with the applicable restrictive covenant obligations, Ms. Widmer will receive the severance payments and benefits payable to executive officers (other than the chief executive officer) under the Severance Plan and the vesting and other treatment due pursuant to the existing terms of her long-term incentive and other awards.
