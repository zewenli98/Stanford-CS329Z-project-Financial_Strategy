Item
8.01     Other Events.
On August 2, 2024, Uber Technologies, Inc. (the “Company”)
announced that Tony West, the Company’s Senior Vice President, Chief Legal Officer and Corporate Secretary, plans to take a temporary
unpaid leave of absence commencing August 17, 2024, to volunteer on a political campaign in his personal capacity. Katie Waitzman, the
Company’s Chief Deputy General Counsel, will assume Mr. West’s duties as General Counsel, and Elizabeth Coleman, the Company’s
Deputy General Counsel and Deputy Corporate Secretary, will assume Mr. West’s duties as Corporate Secretary during such leave of
absence.
