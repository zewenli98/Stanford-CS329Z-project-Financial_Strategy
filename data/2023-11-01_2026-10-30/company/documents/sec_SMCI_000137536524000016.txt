Item 8.01    Other Events
On Februar  y 22, 202  4, Super Micro Computer, Inc. (the “  Company  ”) issued a press release announcing the pricing of its offering of convertible senior notes due 2029 in an aggregate principal amount of $1.5 billion (the “  Notes  ”).
