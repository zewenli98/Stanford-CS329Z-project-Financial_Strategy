Effective December 31, 2025, Truist Financial Corporation ("Truist") has made certain changes and reclassifications to the presentation of its consolidated statements of income to better align financial reporting with Truist's management of its businesses. The unaudited supplemental historical financial information below reflects these changes and reclassifications, which have been applied retrospectively to all periods presented. This information is being provided for illustrative purposes only.
