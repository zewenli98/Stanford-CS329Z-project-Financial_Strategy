ITEM 7.01    Regulation FD Disclosure.
Mike Maguire, Chief Financial Officer of Truist Financial Corporation (“Truist”), will speak at the Barclays Global Financial Services Conference in New York City on Tuesday, September 15, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. ET.
