Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure
On July 27, 2026, the Company announced that it has reached an agreement for a comprehensive resolution of the ovarian talc litigation with the plaintiff firms leading the federal Multi-District Litigation (MDL) and related state court proceedings, conditioned upon, among other things, the express participation of at least of 95% of the remaining claims. The agreement calls for per claim payments, with a $5.5 billion commitment by the Company, and the first payment of no more than $3 billion to be made in 2027 and no additional payments due before 2028.
