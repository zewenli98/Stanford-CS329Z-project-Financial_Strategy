Item 2.01
Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets.
On March 6, 2024, Dominion Energy, Inc. (Dominion Energy) completed the previously announced disposition of Dominion Energy Questar Corporation (the Company), including the East Ohio Gas Company (d/b/a Dominion Energy Ohio) (East Ohio) and related entities, pursuant to the Purchase and Sale Agreement dated as of September 5, 2023 (the Purchase Agreement), by and between Dominion Energy and Enbridge Elephant Holdings, LLC (the Purchaser), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Enbridge Inc., a Canadian corporation (the Transaction). Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, Dominion Energy sold to Purchaser, and Purchaser purchased from Dominion Energy, all of Dominion Energy’s right, title and interest in and to all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock in the Company.
At the closing of the Transaction, Purchaser provided total consideration of approximately $4.3 billion in cash, subject to post-closing adjustments, and the assumption by Purchaser of approximately $2.3 billion of related long-term debt, totaling a value of $6.6 billion for the Transaction.
East Ohio includes Dominion Energy’s regulated natural gas sales, transportation, gathering, storage and distribution operations in Ohio that serves approximately 1.2 million residential customers.
The Purchase Agreement and the Transaction contemplated thereby are more fully described in Dominion Energy’s Current Report on Form  8-K  filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on September 5, 2023, which description is incorporated herein by reference. Such description and the foregoing description of the Transaction and the Purchase Agreement do not purport to be complete and are qualified in their entirety by reference to the full text of the Purchase Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 2.1 hereto and to such Current Report on Form  8-K  and is incorporated herein by reference.
