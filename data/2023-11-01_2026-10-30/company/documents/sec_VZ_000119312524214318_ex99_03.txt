FOR MEDIA:
Press Confirmation Email
Sharing some breaking news this morning - we’ve just announced that we’ve entered into a definitive agreement for Verizon to acquire
Frontier in an  all-cash  transaction valued at $20 billion.
This strategic acquisition will increase
Verizon’s scale with 2.2 million fiber subscribers and extend Verizon’s network reach to 25 million premises across 31 states and Washington, D.C., accelerating the company’s delivery of premium mobility and broadband
connectivity services. And over the long-term, it will also expand Verizon’s intelligent edge network for digital innovations like AI and IoT.
As
you know, Verizon already has an industry-leading value prop in mobility (myPlan) and home internet (myHome) where customers get the best connectivity and exclusive value on content and connected home offerings. Upon close of the
transaction, Verizon will become among the top fiber players in the country, providing premium broadband and mobility services to consumer and small business customers.
Please refer to the press release and graphic attached. In addition, Verizon management will conduct a conference call today, September 5, 2024, at 8:00
AM Eastern Time to discuss this announcement. Access to a live audio webcast and slide presentation will be available on its Investor Relations website, https://www.verizon.com/about/investors. An archive of the webcast will be available on the
website.
Many thanks!
