Item 8.01. Other Events
2025 South Dakota Electric Rate Case
On June 30, 2025, Northern States Power Company, a Minnesota corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Xcel Energy Inc. (“NSP-Minnesota”), filed a request with the South Dakota Public Utilities Commission for a net annual electric rate increase of $44 million (15%). The filing is based on a 2024 historic test year, a requested return on equity of 10.3%, rate base of approximately $1.2 billion and an equity ratio of 52.87%. NSP-Minnesota has requested rates to begin on Jan. 1, 2026. If approved as filed, this rate request would result in an average annual residential bill increase of 3% over the period from 2016-2026.
