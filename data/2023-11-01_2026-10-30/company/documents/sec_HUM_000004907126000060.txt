Item 7.01    Regulation FD Disclosure.
Blackrock Joint Venture
Humana Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release today reporting its entry into a new joint venture arrangement with funds and accounts managed by HPS Investment Partners, a subsidiary of BlackRock, Inc. (“BlackRock”), to manage and operate an existing network of CenterWell Senior Primary Care clinics that currently sit within joint ventures owned by funds affiliated with Welsh, Carson, Anderson & Stowe (“WCAS”) and CenterWell. The new joint venture arrangement will replace CenterWell’s existing joint ventures with WCAS through BlackRock’s acquisition of WCAS’s current equity stake, with certain modifications to the existing terms.
Among other things, the new joint venture does not include a put option exercisable by BlackRock. The new joint venture arrangement will give rise to a derivative instrument that will be recorded on the Company's balance sheet at fair value; however, the Company expects a significant reduction in the liabilities on its balance sheet associated with the new arrangement compared to the put arrangements for the existing joint ventures.
CenterWell will have the option to begin purchasing clinics from the joint venture at its election beginning in 2028. If CenterWell has not acquired all of the joint venture’s clinics by the end of 2033, BlackRock will have the right to elect to exit the joint venture through a sale process for the joint venture’s remaining clinics, subject to CenterWell’s right to submit the first offer to acquire any such remaining clinics. CenterWell has also agreed to certain minimum return protections for BlackRock, resulting in the derivative instrument mentioned above. All in, Humana anticipates a significantly lower cost of capital under the new joint venture, reflecting the maturity of the business.
A copy of the release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
2026 Earnings Guidance
Humana affirms its guidance of at least $9.00 in adjusted earnings per common share (“Adjusted EPS”), for the year ending December 31, 2026 (“FY 2026”). This guidance is consistent with the guidance issued in Humana’s press release dated July 29, 2026 and subsequently affirmed in the Form 8-K’s filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on September 1, 2026 and October 9, 2026.
At this time, the Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of its guidance for FY 2026 Adjusted EPS to FY 2026 diluted earnings per common share (“EPS”), the most directly comparable Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (“GAAP”) financial measure, without unreasonable effort because, at this time, the Company is completing the financial close process for the three and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026. Accordingly, the financial guidance described herein is preliminary, based upon information currently available, and remains subject to change as the company completes its customary closing and review procedures.
The Company does not expect changes to its FY 2026 Adjusted (non-GAAP) EPS guidance. The Company does expect potential changes to its FY 2026 GAAP EPS guidance due to its ongoing value creation and other strategic initiatives including the significant reduction in liabilities related to the removal of the put option related to the existing WCAS joint ventures.
