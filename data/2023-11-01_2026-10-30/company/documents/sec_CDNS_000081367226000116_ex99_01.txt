CFO COMMENTARY
Key Takeaways
• Exceeded Q3 guidance for revenue, non-GAAP operating margin, and non-GAAP EPS
• Raising 2026 revenue growth outlook for the third time this year to ~21%
• Achieved new record backlog of $8.2 billion
Q4 2026 Outlook
• Revenue: $1.695 billion - $1.725 billion
• GAAP operating margin: 30% - 31%
• Non-GAAP operating margin: 44% - 45%
• GAAP EPS: $1.23 - $1.29
• Non-GAAP EPS: $2.19 - $2.25
• Expect to use approximately $400 million to repurchase Cadence shares in Q4
Q3 2026 KEY METRICS
• Revenue: $1.633 billion
• GAAP operating margin: 26.2%
• Non-GAAP operating margin: 44.1%
• GAAP EPS: $1.05
• Non-GAAP EPS: $2.11
• Operating cash flow: $424 million
FY 2026 Outlook
• Revenue: $6.387 billion - $6.417 billion
• GAAP operating margin: 28.2% - 29.2%
• Non-GAAP operating margin: 44.2% - 45.2%
• GAAP EPS: $4.84 - $4.90
• Non-GAAP EPS: $8.37 - $8.43
• Operating cash flow: ~$2.0 billion
• Expect to use more than 50% of free cash flow to repurchase Cadence shares for the year
