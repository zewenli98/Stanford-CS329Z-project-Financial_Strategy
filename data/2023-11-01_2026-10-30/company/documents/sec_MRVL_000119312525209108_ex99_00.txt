Marvell Technology, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend Payment
Santa Clara, Calif. (September 19, 2025)  —  Marvell Technology, Inc.  (NASDAQ: MRVL), today announced a quarterly dividend of $0.06 per
share of common stock payable on October 30, 2025 to shareholders of record as of October 10, 2025.
