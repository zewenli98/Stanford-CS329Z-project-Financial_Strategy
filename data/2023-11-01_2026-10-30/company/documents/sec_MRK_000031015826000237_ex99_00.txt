Paul J. Diaz Elected to Merck & Co., Inc., Rahway, N.J., USA Board of Directors
RAHWAY, N.J., Oct. 13, 2026 – Merck & Co., Inc., Rahway, N.J., USA (NYSE: MRK), known as MSD outside the United States and Canada, today announced that Paul J. Diaz, managing partner at Cressey & Company, will join the Company’s Board of Directors effective Nov. 1, 2026. He will stand for election by the company’s shareholders in May 2027. With the addition of Mr. Diaz, the Merck board will consist of 14 members.
“We are pleased to welcome Paul Diaz to our Board. Paul brings extensive leadership and business expertise in the healthcare industry, and we look forward to his contributions and insights,” said Rob Davis, chairman and chief executive officer.
Mr. Diaz is a managing partner at Cressey & Company, a private investment firm focused on investing in and cultivating high-quality health care firms. He previously served as president and chief executive officer of Myriad Genetics, Inc., a leading genetic testing and precision medicine company. Before that, he served as president and chief executive officer of Kindred Healthcare, a Fortune 500 healthcare services company. Mr. Diaz also serves on the Board of Centene Corporation, the Board of Trustees of Johns Hopkins Medicine and the Board of Visitors of the Georgetown University Law Center. He holds a bachelor’s degree in finance and accounting from American University and a J.D. from Georgetown University.
