Item 8.01.
Other Events.
On November 14, 2023, Airbnb, Inc. (the “Company”) filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) a prospectus supplement to the prospectus included in the Company’s registration statement on Form  S-3ASR  (File No. 333-275550), filed with the SEC on November 14, 2023 (the “Registration Statement”), covering the resale from time to time by certain stockholders of up to an aggregate of 877,062 shares (the “Shares”) of the Company’s Class A common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, to satisfy registration rights the Company granted pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger with the parties affiliated with such stockholders in connection with the Company’s acquisition of a corporation.
A copy of the legal opinion of Latham & Watkins LLP relating to the validity of the Shares is filed herewith as Exhibit 5.1 and is incorporated herein by reference, and is filed with reference to, and is hereby incorporated by reference into, the Registration Statement.
