Item 8.01 Other Events
Effective September 1, 2023, Accenture plc (“Accenture”) revised the reporting of its geographic markets for the movement of its Middle East and Africa market units from Growth Markets to Europe. Accenture’s Europe market is now referred to as its EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Africa) geographic market.
