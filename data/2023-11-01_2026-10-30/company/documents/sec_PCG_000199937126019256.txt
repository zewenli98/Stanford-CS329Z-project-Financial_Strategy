Item
7.01  Regulation FD Disclosure.
PG&E Corporation will hold a webcast and conference call on Wednesday
September 2, 2026, at 8:30 AM Eastern Time, to provide an investor update, including PG&E Corporation’s response to Senate Bill
492. PG&E Corporation does not believe Senate Bill 492 adequately addresses the financing risks created by California’s current
wildfire liability framework. As a result, PG&E Corporation believes it falls short of creating the long-term durability needed to
attract affordable investment to support a safer, more reliable energy system and help keep costs down for customers.
To access the live audio webcast, please visit the PG&E Corporation
Investor Relations website at http://investor.pgecorp.com, under the “News & Events: Events & Presentations” tab.
A replay of the conference call will also be archived at that page. Alternatively, a toll-free replay of the conference call may
be accessed shortly after the live call through September 9, 2026, by dialing (800) 770-2030. The confirmation code 92587 will be required
to access the replay.
