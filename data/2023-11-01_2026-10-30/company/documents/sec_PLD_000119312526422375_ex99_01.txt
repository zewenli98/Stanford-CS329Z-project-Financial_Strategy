Prologis Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Healthy customer demand supports continued momentum
SAN FRANCISCO (October 15, 2026)   – Prologis, Inc. (NYSE: PLD) today announced results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•
Net earnings per diluted share was $0.86, compared with $0.82 for the same period in 2025.
•
Core funds from operations (Core FFO)* per diluted share was $1.60, compared with $1.49 for the same period in 2025.
•
Core FFO, excluding Net Promote Income (Expense)* per diluted share was $1.61, compared with $1.50 for the same period in 2025.
“We executed well on our plan this quarter, signing 60 million square feet of leases and announcing nearly $2.3 billion of transactions,” said Daniel S. Letter, chief executive officer of Prologis. “Customers are running lean on inventory, and excess capacity within their networks has largely cleared. We see healthy demand supported by e-commerce, advanced manufacturing and the build-out of the data center supply chain.”
Letter added: “On SEGRO, shareholder approval was an important milestone in bringing our two companies together. We look forward to completing the transaction and expanding our platform in Europe, increasing the scale at which we can serve customers.”
“Our financial results reflect another quarter of strong operating performance and disciplined capital allocation,” said Timothy D. Arndt, chief financial officer of Prologis. “That performance, together with the strength of our balance sheet, gives us the capacity and flexibility to continue investing in growth even in a higher interest rate environment.”

BALANCE SHEET STRENGTH & LIQUIDITY
During the quarter, the company:
•
Closed, together with its co-investment ventures, an aggregate of $1.3 billion of debt at a weighted average interest rate of 4.0% and a weighted average term of 4.5 years.
As of quarter-end:
•
Total available liquidity was approximately $8.8 billion.
•
Debt-to-Adjusted EBITDA* was 4.5x and debt as a percentage of total market capitalization was 23.9%.
•
The weighted average interest rate on the company’s share of total debt was 3.3%, with a weighted average term of 7.6 years.
•
Forecasted earnings for 2026, 2027 and 2028 are 99%, 98% and 97%, respectively, in USD or hedged through derivative contracts and 96% of Prologis’ equity was in USD.
