Item 8.01.     Other Events
As previously disclosed, on July 1, 2026, S&P Global Inc. (the “Registrant”) completed the separation of its S&P Global Mobility segment (“Mobility”) into a separate, independent public company, Mobility Global Inc. (the “Separation”).
The Registrant is providing the unaudited pro forma condensed consolidated financial statements of the Registrant giving effect to the Separation, which includes (i) the unaudited pro forma condensed consolidated statements of income for the six months ended June 30, 2026 and each of the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024 and 2023, and (ii) the unaudited pro forma condensed consolidated balance sheet as of June 30, 2026, which are filed as Exhibit 99 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated by reference herein.
