T-Mobile  Agrees to Sell $3.5 Billion of Senior Notes
March 24, 2025
BELLEVUE, Wash.—(BUSINESS  WIRE)—T-Mobile  US, Inc. (NASDAQ: TMUS)  (“T-Mobile”)  announced today that  T-Mobile  USA, Inc., its direct wholly-owned
subsidiary  (“T-Mobile  USA” or the “Issuer”), has agreed to sell $1,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 5.125% Senior Notes due 2032 (the “2032 Notes”), $1,000,000,000
aggregate principal amount of its 5.300% Senior Notes due 2035 (the “2035 Notes”) and $1,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 5.875% Senior Notes due 2055 (the “2055 Notes,” and collectively with the 2032 Notes and the
2035 Notes, the “notes”) in a registered public offering.
The offering of the notes is scheduled to close on March 27, 2025, subject to
satisfaction of customary closing conditions.  T-Mobile  USA intends to use the net proceeds from the offering for general corporate purposes, which may include among other things, share repurchases, any
dividends declared by  T-Mobile’s  Board of Directors and refinancing of existing indebtedness on an ongoing basis.
