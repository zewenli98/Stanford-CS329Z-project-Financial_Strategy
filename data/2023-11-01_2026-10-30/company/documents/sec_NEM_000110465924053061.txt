ITEM 5.07    SUBMISSION OF MATTERS TO A VOTE OF SECURITY HOLDERS
On April 24, 2024, Newmont Corporation, a
Delaware Corporation (the “Company”) held its 2024 Annual Meeting of Stockholders. The following matters were voted upon
at the Annual Meeting: (1) the election of Directors; (2) the approval of the advisory resolution on executive compensation;
and (3) ratification of the appointment of Ernst & Young LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting
firm for 2024.
