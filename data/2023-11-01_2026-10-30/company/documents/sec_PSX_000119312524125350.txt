Item 5.02
Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On April 26, 2024, Timothy D. Roberts, Executive Vice President, Midstream and Chemicals of Phillips 66 (the “Company”) provided notice to the Company that he intends to retire, effective June 1, 2024.
Item 7.01
Regulation FD Disclosure.
On May 1, 2024, the Company announced Don A. Baldridge, the current interim CEO of DCP Midstream, LP (“DCP Midstream”), will succeed Tim Roberts as Executive Vice President, Midstream and Chemicals, effective June 1, 2024. Mr. Baldridge joined the Company as part of the DCP Midstream integration and previously served as DCP Midstream’s President of Operations, overseeing the Permian and South business units and other related logistics, marketing, and commercial operations. He has also served in various roles in origination, business development, optimization, contract administration, gas control, and marketing and trading.
