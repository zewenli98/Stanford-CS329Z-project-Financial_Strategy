T-Mobile  Agrees to Sell $2.0 Billion of Senior Notes
January 7, 2026
BELLEVUE, Wash.—(BUSINESS  WIRE)—T-Mobile  US, Inc. (NASDAQ: TMUS)  (“T-Mobile”)  announced today that  T-Mobile  USA, Inc., its direct wholly-owned
subsidiary  (“T-Mobile  USA” or the “Issuer”), has agreed to sell $1,150,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 5.000% Senior Notes due 2036 (the “2036 Notes”) and
$850,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 5.850% Senior Notes due 2056 (the “2056 Notes,” and collectively with the 2036 Notes, the “notes”) in a registered public offering.
The offering of the notes is scheduled to close on January 12, 2026, subject to satisfaction of customary closing
conditions. T-Mobile USA  intends to use the net proceeds from the offering for refinancing existing indebtedness on an ongoing basis, or other general corporate purposes.
