Item 8.01    Other Events
On July 25, 2025, a jury returned a unanimous verdict in favor of CME Group Inc. (“CME Group”) and the Board of Trade of the City of Chicago, Inc. (“CBOT”) in the putative class action that was filed on January 15, 2014 in the Circuit Court of Cook County, Chancery Division. The plaintiffs, certain Class B shareholders of CME Group and Class B members of CBOT, had alleged breach of contract and breach of the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing for violations of their core rights granted in the defendants’ respective Certificates of Incorporation.
