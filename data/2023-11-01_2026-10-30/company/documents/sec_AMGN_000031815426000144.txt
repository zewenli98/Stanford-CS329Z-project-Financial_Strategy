Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
(d) Election of Directors.
On October 26, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Amgen Inc. (the “Company”) appointed David W. Gibbs as a director of the Company, effective immediately.
Mr. Gibbs served as Chief Executive Officer of Yum! Brands, Inc., a leading global restaurant company, from 2020 to September 2025 and as a director of Yum! Brands from 2019 to September 2025. Since October 2025, Mr. Gibbs has served as an advisor to Yum! Brands. Previously, he served as President and Chief Operating Officer of Yum! Brands from August 2019 to December 2019; President, Chief Operating Officer and Chief Financial Officer from January 2019 to August 2019; and President and Chief Financial Officer from 2016 to 2018. Earlier in his career at Yum! Brands, Mr. Gibbs served as Chief Executive Officer of the Pizza Hut Division from 2015 to 2016, President of Pizza Hut U.S. in 2014, and President and Chief Financial Officer of Yum! Restaurants International, Inc. from 2012 to 2013. Mr. Gibbs has served as a director of Under Armour, Inc. since 2021 and is Chair of its Human Capital and Compensation Committee. He has served as a director of PepsiCo, Inc. since 2026 and is a member of its Audit Committee. Mr. Gibbs holds a bachelor’s degree in mathematical science from Johns Hopkins University and a Master of Business Administration degree from the Fuqua School of Business at Duke University.
Mr. Gibbs will serve as a member of the Board’s Audit Committee and its Compensation and Management Development Committee. Following the appointment of Mr. Gibbs, the Board will be composed of 13 directors, 12 of whom are independent.
There are no transactions between Mr. Gibbs (or any member of his immediate family) and the Company (or any of its subsidiaries) that would be reportable under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K, and there is no arrangement or understanding between Mr. Gibbs and any other persons or entities pursuant to which Mr. Gibbs was appointed as a director of the Company.
Upon the effective date of his appointment to the Board, Mr. Gibbs will be entitled to the Company’s standard non-employee director compensation and, thus, will receive a pro-rated portion of the (i) annual cash retainer of $115,000 and (ii) committee member meeting retainers of $12,500, in each case through December 31, 2026. Under the Company’s director equity incentive program, non-employee directors receive an annual grant of fully vested restricted stock units with a grant date fair value of $220,000 (rounded down to the nearest whole number of shares), measured by the closing market price of a share of the Company’s common stock on the date of grant (the date of the annual meeting of stockholders). Accordingly, Mr. Gibbs will receive a pro-rated portion (7/12) of the annual grant of fully vested restricted stock units on the date that is two business days after the release of the Company’s 2026 third quarter earnings. In accordance with the Company’s policy, Mr. Gibbs will also be entitled to reimbursement of his expenses incurred in connection with attendance at Board and committee meetings and conferences with the Company’s senior management.
