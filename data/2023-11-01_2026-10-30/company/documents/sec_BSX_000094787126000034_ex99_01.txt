Transaction Details
Expect to complete the transaction in 2026
Transaction Structure
Transaction consideration reflects a total equity value of $15B to be funded in approx. $11B in cash and approx. $4B in Boston Scientific stock, resulting in a 73% cash, 27% stock mix
Based on Boston Scientific’s 10-day VWAP1 ending January 13, 2026, this will result in an equity issuance of approx. 41M shares, which was fixed at the time of signing
Sources of financing
Expect to finance ~$11B cash portion of transaction with a combination of cash on hand and new debt
Nature of debt will be finalized between sign and close
Capital Allocation
Expect to maintain strong credit ratings
Provides flexibility to execute capital allocation priorities of further tuck-in M&A followed by opportunistic share repurchase
Approval Process
Subject to approval from Penumbra shareholders and customary closing conditions
Approved by both companies’ boards of directors
