Item 1.01    Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement
On January 26, 2024, Super Micro Computer, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a purchase and sale agreement (the “Purchase Agreement’) with Caracol Property Owner LLC (the “Seller”) to purchase real estate from the Seller comprising approximately 19.72 acres of land and 293,906 square feet of buildings and improvements located in close proximity to the Company’s headquarters space in San Jose, California (the “Property”) “as is” for an aggregate purchase price of $80 million, subject to certain adjustments to be determined at closing. Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company made a deposit $10 million, which amount is non-refundable except in limited circumstances. The transaction, currently expected to close in February 2024, is subject to customary closing conditions. The Seller previously obtained, and the Company is acquiring under the Purchase Agreement, discretionary entitlements for a potential redevelopment of the Property with phased construction of seven office buildings totaling approximately 1.9 million square feet and two parking structures totaling approximately 1.6 million square feet.
The Company currently leases a portion of the Property for warehouse space.
The foregoing description of the Purchase Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Purchase Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference herein  .
