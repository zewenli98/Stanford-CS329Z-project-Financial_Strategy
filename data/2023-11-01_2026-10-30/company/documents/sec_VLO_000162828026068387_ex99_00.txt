Valero to Acquire Former Houston Refinery from LyondellBasell
•  Plans to Restart 165,000 BPD of Throughput Capacity by Year-End 2027
•  Opportunity to Invest in High-Complexity, Heavy Sour Crude Processing
SAN ANTONIO, October 30, 2026 – Valero Energy Corporation (NYSE: VLO, “Valero”) announced today that its wholly owned subsidiary, Valero Refining-Texas, L.P., entered into a definitive agreement to acquire all the outstanding equity interests of LYB Houston Hub Holdings LLC (“LYB Houston”) from a subsidiary of LyondellBasell Industries N.V. (“Lyondell”) for a purchase price of $320.5 million, subject to closing adjustments. Closing of the transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended, and is expected to occur by the end of 2026. LYB Houston indirectly owns an approximately 700-acre facility adjacent to Valero’s Houston refinery on the Houston Ship Channel in Texas that operated as a petroleum refinery. Valero currently expects to initially restart 165,000 BPD of light sweet crude processing capacity by year-end 2027. The acquisition also provides the opportunity to later invest in high-complexity, heavy sour crude processing.
“This acquisition is a unique opportunity to strategically expand Valero’s U.S. Gulf Coast footprint at a fraction of replacement value and enhance the long-term strength of our refining system,” said Lane Riggs, Chairman, CEO and President of Valero. “It is expected to provide a compelling financial return, consistent with Valero’s disciplined capital allocation framework, while generating attractive commercial optimization opportunities and supporting future investment in high-complexity, heavy sour crude oil processing.”
Valero has prepared a presentation related to the transaction, which will be posted on its Investor Relations website and furnished as an exhibit in a Current Report on Form 8-K.
About Valero
Valero Energy Corporation, through its subsidiaries (collectively, Valero), is a multinational manufacturer and marketer of petroleum-based and low-carbon liquid transportation fuels and petrochemical products, and sells its products primarily in the United States (U.S.), Canada, the United Kingdom (U.K.), Ireland, and Latin America. Valero operates 14 petroleum refineries
located in the U.S., Canada, and the U.K. with a combined throughput capacity of approximately 3.0 million barrels per day. Valero is a joint venture member in Diamond Green Diesel Holdings LLC, which produces low-carbon fuels including renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), with a production capacity of approximately 1.2 billion gallons per year in the U.S. Gulf Coast region. See the annual report on Form 10-K for more information on SAF. Valero also owns 12 ethanol plants located in the U.S. Mid-Continent region with a combined production capacity of approximately 1.7 billion gallons per year. Valero manages its operations through its Refining, Renewable Diesel, and Ethanol segments. Please visit investorvalero.com for more information.
