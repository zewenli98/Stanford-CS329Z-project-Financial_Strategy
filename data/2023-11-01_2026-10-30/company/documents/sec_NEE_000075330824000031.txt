Item 8.01 Other Events
On June 3, 2024, Florida Power & Light Company sold $750 million principal amount of its 5.15% First Mortgage Bonds, Series due June 15, 2029, $750 million principal amount of its 5.30% First Mortgage Bonds, Series due June 15, 2034 and $850 million principal amount of its 5.60% First Mortgage Bonds, Series due June 15, 2054 (collectively, the Offered Bonds).
