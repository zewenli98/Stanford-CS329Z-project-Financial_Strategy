Item 7.01    Regulation FD Disclosure.
United Airlines Holdings, Inc. ("UAL"), the holding company whose subsidiary is United Airlines, Inc. ("United," and together with UAL, the "Company"), is providing an update regarding its financial outlook for second quarter 2024. The Company continues to expect second quarter 2024 adjusted diluted earnings per share  1   to be between $3.75 and $4.25.
