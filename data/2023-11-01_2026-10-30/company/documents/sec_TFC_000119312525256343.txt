ITEM 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
Steven C. Voorhees, a member of the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Truist Financial Corporation (“Truist”), has notified Truist of his decision to retire from the Board effective December 31, 2025. Mr. Voorhees’s retirement is for personal reasons and not due to any disagreement with Truist on any matter relating to its operations, policies, or practices. The Board and Truist’s management express their deep gratitude to Mr. Voorhees for his purposeful service and many significant contributions to Truist during his tenure as a director.
