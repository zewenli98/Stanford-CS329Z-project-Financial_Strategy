KLA Declares Regular Cash Dividend
MILPITAS, Calif., Nov. 2, 2023 — The KLA Corporation (NASDAQ: KLAC) Board of Directors today declared a quarterly cash dividend of $1.45 per share on its
common stock, payable on Dec. 1, 2023, to KLA shareholders of record as of the close of business on Nov. 15, 2023.
