Item 8.01 Other Events.
On January 13, 2023, Dominion Energy, Inc. (the Company) entered into a  $2.5 billion 364-day term  loan facility (the facility). On January 12, 2024, the facility was amended to extend the maturity date to July 12, 2024. In connection with the strategic business review announced in November 2022, the Company entered into agreements in September 2023 to sell all of its regulated gas distribution operations, except for the regulated gas distribution operations in South Carolina, to wholly-owned subsidiaries of Enbridge Inc. The Company expects to use all  after-tax  proceeds from these sales to repay debt, including the facility.
