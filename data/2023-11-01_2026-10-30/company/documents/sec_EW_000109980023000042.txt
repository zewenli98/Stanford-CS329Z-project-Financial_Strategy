Item 7.01.    Regulation FD Disclosure.
On December 7, 2023, Edwards Lifesciences Corporation, a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), issued a press release in connection with the Company’s previously announced 2023 Investor Conference (the “2023 Investor Conference”).
A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. In accordance with General Instruction B.2 of Form 8-K, the information in this Item 7.01, including Exhibit 99.1, shall not be deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”), or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
Item 8.01.    Other Events.
Strategic Spin-Off of Critical Care
On December 7, 2023, in connection with the 2023 Investor Conference, the Company announced its intention to complete a tax-free spin-off of its Critical Care product group at the end of 2024. Katie Szyman, Corporate Vice President of Critical Care since 2015, will be Chief Executive Officer of the new company upon completion of the transaction. More information regarding the planned spin-off will be available in 2024, and the company is planning a  mid-year Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) Form 10 submission.
Share Repurchase Authorization
Also on December 7, 2023, in connection with the 2023 Investor Conference, the Company announced that its Board of Directors approved $1 billion of additional share repurchase authorization.
