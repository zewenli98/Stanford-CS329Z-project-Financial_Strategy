T-Mobile  Agrees to Sell $2.5 Billion of Senior Notes
September 23, 2024
BELLEVUE, Wash.—(BUSINESS  WIRE)—T-Mobile  US, Inc. (NASDAQ: TMUS)  (“T-Mobile”)  announced today that  T-Mobile  USA, Inc., its direct wholly-owned
subsidiary  (“T-Mobile  USA” or the “Issuer”), has agreed to sell $700,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 4.200% Senior Notes due 2029 (the “2029 Notes”), $900,000,000
aggregate principal amount of its 4.700% Senior Notes due 2035 (the “2035 Notes”) and $900,000,000 aggregate principal amount of its 5.250% Senior Notes due 2055 (the “2055 Notes,” and collectively with the 2029 Notes and the
2035 Notes, the “notes”) in a registered public offering.
The offering of the notes is scheduled to close on September 26, 2024, subject
to satisfaction of customary closing conditions.  T-Mobile  USA intends to use the net proceeds from the offering for general corporate purposes, which may include among other things, share repurchases, any
dividends declared by  T-Mobile’s  Board of Directors and refinancing of existing indebtedness on an ongoing basis.
