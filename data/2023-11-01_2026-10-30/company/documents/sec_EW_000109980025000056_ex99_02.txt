EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES ANNOUNCES $500 MILLION ACCELERATED SHARE REPURCHASE
IRVINE, Calif.,   Aug. 19, 2025     – Edwards Lifesciences (NYSE: EW) today announced that it has executed an accelerated share repurchase agreement ("ASR") to repurchase $500 million of Edwards' common stock.  With this transaction, Edwards' has repurchased more than $800 million of shares in 2025.
Under the terms of this ASR, Edwards will receive an initial delivery of approximately 5 million shares and the remainder at the maturity of the ASR. The final number of shares to be repurchased will be based on the volume weighted average share price during the term of the agreement. The company is funding the share repurchase under the ASR with its existing cash.
Following this repurchase, Edwards has approximately $600 million remaining in its share repurchase authorization approved by its Board of Directors.
