Section 1 - Registrant’s Business and Operations
Item 1.01 –       Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement
On October 21, 2026, Workday, Inc. (“Workday”)
entered into an Underwriting Agreement (the “Underwriting Agreement”) with Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, BofA Securities,
Inc., Barclays Capital Inc., and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, as representatives of the several underwriters named therein. The Underwriting
Agreement provides for the issuance and sale by Workday of $2.5 billion aggregate principal amount of senior notes, consisting of $750.0
million aggregate principal amount of 5.700% senior notes due 2029 (the “2029 Senior Notes”), $500.0 million aggregate principal
amount of 6.000% senior notes due 2031 (the “2031 Senior Notes”), $750.0 million aggregate principal amount of 6.300% senior
notes due 2033 (the “2033 Senior Notes”), and $500.0 million aggregate principal amount of 6.500% senior notes due 2036 (the
“2036 Senior Notes,” and together with the 2029 Senior Notes, the 2031 Senior Notes, and the 2033 Senior Notes, the “Notes”),
in an underwritten public offering (the “Offering”).
The Underwriting Agreement contains customary
representations, warranties, and covenants. These representations, warranties, and covenants are not representations of factual information
to investors about Workday or its subsidiaries, and the sale of any Notes pursuant to the Underwriting Agreement is not a representation
that there has not been any change in the condition of Workday. The foregoing description of the terms of the Underwriting Agreement is
not complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by reference to, the complete terms and conditions of the Underwriting Agreement,
which is filed as Exhibit 1.1 hereto and is incorporated by reference herein.
On October 23, 2026, Workday completed its issuance
and sale of the Notes pursuant to the Underwriting Agreement and an indenture, dated as of April 1, 2022 (the “Base Indenture”),
between Workday and U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association, as trustee, together with an officer’s certificate, dated October
23, 2026 (the “Officer’s Certificate,” and, together with the Base Indenture, the “Indenture”). The Notes
were issued and sold pursuant to Workday’s effective shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (Registration No. 333-296348) filed
with the Securities and Exchange Commission on May 29, 2026, including the base prospectus contained therein, as supplemented by a related
preliminary prospectus supplement, dated October 21, 2026, and a final prospectus supplement, dated October 21, 2026.
Workday estimates that the net proceeds from the
Offering will be approximately $2.47 billion, after deducting the underwriting discount and estimated offering expenses payable by Workday.
Workday intends to use the net proceeds from the Offering to redeem, repay, repurchase, or otherwise retire the $1.0 billion in aggregate
principal amount of its 3.500% notes due April 1, 2027 at or before their maturity and to use any remaining net proceeds for general corporate
purposes.
The
Notes are senior unsecured obligations of Workday and rank equally with all existing and future unsecured and unsubordinated indebtedness
of Workday. The 2029 Senior Notes will mature on October 23, 2029, and bear interest at a fixed rate of 5.700% per annum. The 2031 Senior
Notes will mature on October 23, 2031, and bear interest at a fixed rate of 6.000% per annum. The 2033 Senior Notes will mature on October
23, 2033, and bear interest at a fixed rate of 6.300% per annum. The 2036 Senior Notes will mature on October 23, 2036, and bear interest
at a fixed rate of 6.500% per annum. Interest on each series of Notes is payable semi-annually in arrears on April 23 and October 23 of
each year, commencing on April 23, 2027.  The Notes are redeemable at the option of Workday, in whole or in part, at any time and
from time to time, as described in the Indenture, at the applicable redemption prices specified in the forms of Note included in Exhibits
4.3, 4.4, 4.5, and 4.6 hereto (together, the “Forms of Note”).
In addition, if a Change of Control Triggering
Event (as defined in the Forms of Note) occurs with respect to a series of Notes, Workday will be required, subject to certain exceptions,
to make an offer to purchase the Notes of such series at a price in cash equal to 101% of the aggregate principal amount of the Notes
to be purchased, plus accrued and unpaid interest, if any, to, but excluding, the repurchase date.  The
Indenture also contains certain other covenants (including limited covenants restricting the ability of Workday and its restricted subsidiaries
to create liens and enter into sale and lease-back transactions), events of default, and other customary provisions.
The foregoing description of the terms of the
Notes is not complete and is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by reference to, the complete terms and conditions of the Base
Indenture and the Officer’s Certificate (including the Forms of Note), which are filed or incorporated by reference as Exhibits
4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5, and 4.6 hereto and are incorporated by reference herein. In connection with the issuance of the Notes, Fenwick
& West LLP provided Workday with the legal opinion and consent attached hereto as Exhibit 5.1 and Exhibit 23.1, respectively.
