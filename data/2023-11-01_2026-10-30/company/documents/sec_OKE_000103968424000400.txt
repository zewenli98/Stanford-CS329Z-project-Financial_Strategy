Item 8.01     Other Events
On Nov. 19, 2024, we issued a press release announcing the entry into a definitive agreement with DT Midstream, Inc. (DT Midstream) pursuant to which, among other things, we will sell to DT Midstream three of our wholly owned interstate natural gas pipeline systems for a total cash consideration of $1.2 billion subject to customary adjustments.
