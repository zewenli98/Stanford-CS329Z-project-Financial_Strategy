Item 8.01.     Other Events.
As previously reported, on August 29, 2023, 3  M Company (“3M” or the “Company”) and 3M’s wholly-owned subsidiary Aearo Technologies LLC (together with certain of its related entities, the “Aearo Entities”) entered into a settlement arrangement in connection with the litigation and alleged claims involving the Combat Arms Earplugs (CAE) sold or manufactured by the Aearo Entities and/or 3M, including the Combat Arms Settlement Agreement for Wave Cases (Master Settlement Agreement III), dated August 29, 2023, between (1) 3M and Aearo Technologies, LLC and (2) the named Plaintiff’s Leadership in re Combat Arms Earplug Products Liability Litigation, MDL No. 2885, U.S.D.C. for the Northern District of Florida, and (3) the named Plaintiffs’ Leadership in the Minnesota coordinated state court action pending in the 4th Judicial District, County of Hennepin, Minnesota, File No. 27-CV-19-19916 (the “MSA III”).
On January 15, 2024,   3M issued a press release (filed as Exhibit 99.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference) announcing that the MSA III timing has been amended and that the Company will accelerate the   payment of $253.1 million due for these cases to on or before January 31, 2024, in keeping with the Company’s commitment to resolve these claims.
