Charter Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Series A Cumulative
Redeemable Preferred Stock
Stamford, CT – September 15, 2026 – Charter Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHTR, CHTRP) (the “Company”) today announced that its Board of Directors declared the regular quarterly cash dividend payable to holders of its Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock (the “Charter Preferred Stock”) (NASDAQ: CHTRP). The per share amount of the quarterly cash dividend will be $0.43750001, payable in cash on October 15, 2026 to holders of record of the Charter Preferred Stock at the close of business on September 30, 2026.
As previously disclosed, 7,183,812 shares of Charter Preferred Stock were issued in connection with the acquisition of Liberty Broadband Corporation (“Liberty Broadband”).  At the effective time of the acquisition, each share of Liberty Broadband Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock was converted into one newly issued share of Charter Preferred Stock.  The Charter Preferred Stock is entitled to 1/3 of a vote per share, has a liquidation preference of $25 per share plus accrued but unpaid dividends, pays dividends quarterly at a 7% annual rate, and is mandatorily redeemable on March 9, 2039.
About Charter
Charter Communications, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHTR, CHTRP) is the leading broadband and video company in the nation and the fastest growing mobile provider in its footprint, with services available to more than 70 million homes and small to large businesses across 45 states through its Spectrum brand. Founded in 1993, Charter has evolved from providing cable TV to streaming, and from high-speed Internet to a converged broadband, WiFi and mobile experience. Over the Spectrum Fiber Broadband Network and supported by our 100% U.S.-based employees, the Company offers Seamless Connectivity and Entertainment with Spectrum Internet®, Mobile, TV and Voice products.
