Item 7.01 — Regulation FD Disclosure.
On September 18,  2024, T-Mobile US,  Inc. (the “Company”) hosted a Capital Markets Day event. Members of the Company’s senior leadership team discussed the Company’s plans to leverage its industry-leading network to unlock future growth from its existing and new businesses over the next three years.
The event is accessible via webcast on the Company’s Investor Relations website  at http://investor.t-mobile.com. A  replay of the webcast along with presentation materials will be available on the Investor Relations website at the conclusion of the event.
A copy of the press release relating to the Capital Markets Day is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
