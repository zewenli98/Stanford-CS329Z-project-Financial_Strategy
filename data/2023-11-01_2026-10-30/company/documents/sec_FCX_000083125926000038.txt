Item 8.01.   Other Events.
On September 1, 2026, PT Freeport Indonesia (PTFI), Freeport-McMoRan Inc.’s 48.76%-owned subsidiary, amended and restated its $1.75 billion, senior unsecured revolving credit facility to, among other things, extend the maturity date under the facility from November 2028 to September 2031. As of September 1, 2026, PTFI had $250 million in borrowings outstanding under its revolving credit facility. PTFI’s revolving credit facility is available for its general corporate purposes.
