Access to specialized, accessible care is becoming a bigger part of the Walmart associate experience. 2027 Enhancements Associate Stories Associate Benefits (Four)ward Thinking Click Here To Navigate Close Navigation Main Navigation Page Navigation 2027 Enhancements Associate Stories Associate Benefits (Four)ward Thinking 2027 Enhancements As the employer of the U.S.’s largest private workforce committed to supporting the health and well-being of our associates, Walmart is taking action. Starting January 1, 2027, we’re rolling out key enhancements to our benefits package to provide affordable and accessible quality care options for our U.S. associates. Zero Dollar Prescriptions We’re rolling out a new Zero Dollar prescription tier for associates. The new Zero Dollar tier prioritizes medications at lower costs, with more than 250 generic, brand-name and specialty medications, including some insulin and inhalers. Rare Conditions Center of Excellence We’re expanding our Centers of Excellence (COE) with a new Rare Conditions COE , providing associates with no-cost access to specialists for multiple sclerosis, Parkinson’s disease and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) in collaboration with facilities like Mayo Clinic and Cleveland Clinic. Associates and dependents enrolled in most Walmart medical plans who are either being evaluated or have been diagnosed will be able to connect in-person with a specialist for an evaluation and treatment review at no cost. As with all our COEs, travel is also covered for the patient and an adult caregiver. Paid Neonatal Intensive Care Unit Leave One in ten births require admission to a neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). To ensure our associates have the time to focus on what matters most, we’re establishing a dedicated, paid NICU leave . Beginning November 17, 2026, this will provide a maximum of 12 weeks of paid NICU leave for eligible associates with children who are admitted to a NICU during the first 28 days after birth and actively receiving inpatient care. This stackable benefit can be combined with other time off and leave benefits, including maternity and parental paid leaves. When combined, frontline associates can receive up to 28 weeks of paid leave when their birth journey includes NICU admission. We are excited to share upcoming changes to our benefits during this Annual Enrollment season. We're adding even more ways for associates and their families to live better through comprehensive and affordable healthcare and benefits. Donna Morris Executive Vice President and Chief People Officer, Walmart Inc. Tasheka’s Story: Cancer Center of Excellence “I am alive today because of Walmart’s Centers of Excellence.” Tasheka Saunders, Walmart Distribution Center Administrative Assistant In 2023, Tasheka was told she’d need to undergo a full mastectomy to remove cancer that had spread. That’s when she heard about Walmart’s Centers of Excellence (COE) program , connecting associates with some of the best specialists and facilities in the U.S. — at no cost with most Walmart medical plans. Through the Cancer COE, Mayo Clinic provided her with an expert second opinion, updated treatment plan, and treatment. Today, she’s cancer free. It provides personalized care through wearable devices, like Wayne’s glucose monitor and smartwatch. In just the first year of participation in the program, Wayne lost 50 pounds and kept his blood pressure and cholesterol down, improving his insulin resistance and liver function. After talking with his People Partner about ways to help, Justin called Walmart’s mental health resource — and an on-site grief counselor arrived the next day. Liz Brown, Sam’s Club Home Office Sr. Manager, Business Analysis & Insights When Liz joined Walmart, she was a single mom who was slowly starting to realize she wasn’t feeling like herself. After undergoing testing, she learned she had Turner Syndrome, a genetic condition that may call for additional neuro evaluations. For Liz, it looked like a long road to wellness that could take multiple waitlists to finally be tested — until Walmart’s neurodiversity benefit stepped in. The benefit allowed her to receive a virtual neuropsychological evaluation, which averages about a four-week waitlist compared to the year Liz was originally quoted.
